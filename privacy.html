ReClaire — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026 Last Updated: August 28, 2026

ReClaire ("we") operates the ReClaire web-scraping and Markdown conversion platform, including our website, APIs, and associated services (collectively, the "Service"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with global data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information, as well as your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and encrypted password credentials created upon registration.
  • Onboarding & Survey Data: Voluntary responses collected during onboarding (e.g., how you found ReClaire, user role/profession).
  • Payment Information: Payment card details, billing address, and transaction metadata. Note: We do not process or store raw payment card numbers on our servers. All payments are processed by PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways (e.g., Stripe).

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Token transactions, request volume, API logs, timestamped action logs, and endpoint interaction data.
  • Technical & Network Data: IP addresses, browser types, user-agent strings, operating systems, and device descriptors.

C. Web Scraping & Input Data

  • Target URLs & Rendered Data: Webpage addresses (URLs) submitted by users for scraping, HTML content, extracted Markdown output, and temporary execution logs.
  • Data Minimization: ReClaire functions as a transient conversion tool. Scraped content is held in temporary caching memory to serve output back to the user and is automatically purged according to our retention schedules.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process personal data strictly under valid legal bases:

  • To Provide and Manage the Service: Creating user accounts, authenticating access via JSON Web Tokens (JWT), processing credit/token deductions, and serving API outputs. (Legal Basis: Contract Performance)
  • Payment Processing & Fraud Prevention: Handling billing, subscriptions, and preventing API credit abuse or unauthorized access. (Legal Basis: Legal Obligation and Legitimate Interests)
  • Service Optimization: Monitoring server performance, debugging Playwright/headless rendering issues, and analyzing system capacity. (Legal Basis: Legitimate Interests)
  • AI Processing: Sending requested webpage text to AI models (such as Google Gemini AI) solely for downstream text cleaning or summarization when requested by the user. (Legal Basis: Contract Performance)

3. Data Sharing & Sub-processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with vetted third-party service providers (sub-processors) necessary to run ReClaire:

  • Infrastructure & Hosting Providers: Render (Backend API hosting), GitHub Pages (Frontend static hosting).
  • Payment Processors: PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways for subscription billing and token sales.
  • AI Infrastructure Providers: Google Cloud / Gemini AI APIs for generative text handling.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, subpoena, or valid government order, we may disclose account information to comply with legal obligations.

4. Payment & Transaction Security

  • PCI-DSS Compliance: All payment workflows are handled by specialized, PCI-DSS compliant payment infrastructure. No credit card, debit card, or sensitive payment instrument numbers are handled by or stored on ReClaire's databases.
  • Token Balance Ledger: We maintain an internal ledger mapping user IDs to available token balances and transaction logs to enforce balance checks and top-up operations.

5. Security Measures

We implement robust technical and organizational security controls to safeguard your data, including:

  • Encryption in Transit: All traffic between your browser, our API, and third-party integrations is secured using TLS 1.2/1.3 encryption protocols.
  • Password Hashing: User passwords are stored using salted PBKDF2 key derivation algorithms. Plaintext passwords are never logged or stored.
  • Stateless Auth Security: Authentication relies on signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) using secure server-side secrets.
  • Data Isolation: User database records and token logs are segmented in secure database environments.

6. Web Scraping Compliance & User Obligations

When using ReClaire to scrape third-party websites:

  • User Responsibility: Users are solely responsible for ensuring that URLs submitted to ReClaire do not collect protected Personal Identifiable Information (PII) without a valid legal basis.
  • Prohibited Scraping: Users must not utilize ReClaire to harvest credentials, bypass paywalls, scrape financial records, or target unauthorized private web resources.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Account Data: Held as long as your account remains active.
  • Billing Records: Retained for up to 7 years to satisfy corporate tax and regulatory accounting rules.
  • Scraped Data Caches: Automatically purged from active system memory within 24 to 72 hours.
  • Account Deletion: Users may request permanent deletion of their account and associated metadata by contacting us.

8. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your region (e.g., EU/EEA, UK, California), you possess the following legal rights:

  • Right to Access / Know: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct incomplete or inaccurate account details.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your account and personal identifiers.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Data Sales: ReClaire does not sell personal data.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our privacy response team.
